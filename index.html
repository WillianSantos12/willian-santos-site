02 Research Ongoing
Water · Matter · Environment

My practice emerges from a curiosity to interpret life through the alchemy of painting: the transformation of water, matter, light and pigment into images that remain between presence and disappearance.

Water is a recurring element within this research, extending from its visible surface toward deeper and less accessible dimensions. Across different bodies of work, oceans and ecosystems become fields through which perception encounters what can be seen, sensed and imagined.

The process itself is central. Layers of paint are built, altered, erased and rebuilt. Materials accumulate and transform, allowing errors, discoveries and unexpected relationships to become part of the work.

03 Studio Process

The work begins before the image.

The studio is a space of continuous testing, where painting develops through the encounter between material, gesture and perception.

Acrylic, spray, encaustic, pigments and natural elements are combined in processes that allow the surface to accumulate different states of density, luminosity and opacity.

Rather than illustrating a predetermined image, the work develops through a dialogue between what is controlled and what emerges during the making.

04 About Biography
Willian Santos

Brazilian visual artist based in Florianópolis.

Willian Santos was born in Curitiba, Brazil, in 1985 and lives and works in Florianópolis. He graduated in Visual Arts from Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná in 2009.

With more than nineteen years of practice, his research investigates painting as a material and perceptual field, bringing together water, matter, environment and the visible and invisible forces that sustain life.

His work has been presented in exhibitions and institutional contexts in Brazil, including projects connected to museums, galleries and contemporary art spaces.

02 Research
The image emerges through a continuous encounter between matter, perception and transformation.

My research is driven by the curiosity to interpret life and by the alchemy present in the making of painting.

I am interested in a world revealed through two complementary paths: the path of the eye and the path of the heart.

Water is a central element within this investigation. Across oceans, ecosystems and different states of matter, it becomes a symptom of the presence of visible and invisible forces that sustain life.

The process is at the center of my practice. It is a self-provoked path of errors and discoveries, in which layers of paint continuously die and are reborn.

03 Process

The work begins before the image.

Painting is understood as a field of experimentation in which materials, gestures and perception remain in constant transformation.

I investigate the possibilities of matter through successive layers, allowing accidents, decisions and discoveries to become part of the construction of the image.

What emerges is not simply a representation, but an attempt to approach a state in which matter, image and meaning occupy the same field of presence.

04 About
Willian Santos

Born in Curitiba in 1985, Willian Santos lives and works in Florianópolis, Brazil.

He holds a Bachelor of Visual Arts from Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná, completed in 2009, and has worked in the visual arts for more than 19 years.

His practice develops through painting, drawing, installation and material experimentation, investigating relationships between water, matter, ecosystems and perception.

Since 2009, Santos has also developed a sustained practice with encaustic, combining beeswax, pigments and other materials in a process that explores density, transformation and the relationship between the visible and the sensible.

01 / Research Ongoing

The image emerges through a continuous encounter between matter, perception and transformation.

My practice begins with a curiosity to interpret life through the alchemy of painting — the transformation of water, matter, light and pigment into images that remain between presence and disappearance.

Water occupies a central position in this research, extending from its visible surface toward deeper and less accessible dimensions. The oceans and their ecosystems become fields through which perception encounters what can be seen, sensed and imagined.

The process is fundamental to the work. Layers of paint are built, altered, erased and rebuilt. What appears as an image is therefore also the record of a material encounter: a succession of decisions, discoveries, failures and transformations.

02 / Process

The work begins before the image.

In the studio, painting develops through an open dialogue between material, gesture and perception.

Acrylic, spray, encaustic, pigments and natural elements are combined in processes that allow surfaces to accumulate different states of density, luminosity and opacity.

Rather than illustrating a predetermined image, the work develops through the encounter between what is controlled and what emerges during the making.

Willian Santos was born in Curitiba, Brazil, in 1985 and lives and works in Florianópolis. He graduated in Visual Arts from Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná in 2009.

With more than nineteen years of artistic practice, his research investigates painting as a material and perceptual field, bringing together water, matter, environment and the visible and invisible forces that sustain life.

His practice encompasses painting and material experimentation, including acrylic, spray, encaustic and natural elements. His work has been presented in galleries, exhibitions and institutional contexts in Brazil.

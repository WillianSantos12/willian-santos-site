Research

My research investigates the alchemy of water, matter and environment, approaching painting as a field in which visible and invisible forces become perceptible.

Water occupies a central position in this investigation — from its surfaces and movements to the greater densities and depths of the oceans.

Rather than representing a specific landscape, the work approaches the mystery of life through material processes, perception and the transformation of matter.

Layers of paint are built, transformed, obscured and revealed. The process becomes a way of following the eye and the heart, allowing matter itself to participate in the construction of the image.

About

Willian Santos (Curitiba, Brazil, 1985) is a visual artist based in Florianópolis, Brazil. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Visual Arts from Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná, completed in 2009.

His practice encompasses painting, encaustic, acrylic, spray paint and installation, with an ongoing investigation into material transformation, water, ecosystems and the relationship between perception and the environment.

Since 2009, encaustic has been an important part of his material research, alongside contemporary painting processes that combine accumulation, removal and transformation.

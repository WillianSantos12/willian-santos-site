Water, ecosystems and the transformation of materials are recurring points of departure in Willian Santos’ practice. His research moves between what becomes visible and what remains sensed: density, luminosity, sediment, flow and the forces that sustain life. The process is central. Layers of paint are built, transformed, erased and renewed. The image emerges through this continuous negotiation between material and perception — between the path of the eye and the path of the heart. 03 / About Willian Santos (Curitiba, 1985) lives and works in Florianópolis, Brazil. He has worked in the visual arts for more than 19 years. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Visual Arts from Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná (2009), having begun his formal art studies in 2006. His practice encompasses painting and material research, including acrylic, spray and encaustic. Across different bodies of work, he investigates water, ecosystems, environment and the alchemy of materials. 04 / CV EducationB.A. Visual Arts — Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná, 2009 PracticePainting · Encaustic · Acrylic · Material research Selected galleriesZipper · Mamute · Ybakatu PublicationWillian Santos — Work in Progress Selected exhibitions, collections and publications can be expanded here. 05 / Contact For exhibitions, acquisitions and collaborations. contato@williansantos.comInstagram ↗ © Willian SantosFlorianópolis · Brazil
